Inside our first Eco-Club training day

Home / Blog / Eco-Club Training Day
← Back to Blog

Our first Eco-Club training session brought together a small group of students eager to take environmental action into their own hands. Rather than starting with lectures, the day was built entirely around hands-on activity.

Morning: understanding the problem

The session opened with a short walk around the school compound, where students identified where waste tends to pile up and why. Seeing the problem firsthand, rather than just hearing about it, set the tone for the rest of the day.

Afternoon: building the skills

Students then split into small teams to practice waste segregation, learn the basics of composting, and design a simple clean-up rota for their own classrooms. Each team presented their plan to the group at the end of the session.

Why hands-on matters

We've found that students retain far more from a single afternoon of doing than from weeks of being told. Giving young people real responsibility — a bin to manage, a rota to run — turns awareness into habit.

This pilot session is the model we're now using to roll out Eco-Clubs across more schools. If your school is interested in hosting one, get in touch — we'd love to bring this training to your students.
