Get ready: our next tree-planting drive

Home / Blog / Tree-Planting Drive
← Back to Blog

Our next community tree-planting drive is coming up, and we're inviting volunteers, students, and families to join us for a morning of planting, learning, and building greener neighborhoods together.

What to expect

The day will start with a short briefing on where and why we're planting in this particular area, followed by hands-on planting in small teams. No experience is needed — Eco-Guard volunteers will guide every step.

What to bring

  • Comfortable outdoor clothing and closed shoes
  • A reusable water bottle
  • Gloves, if you have a pair (extra sets will be available)

Why it matters

Beyond improving green cover, tree-planting days support the sanitation and awareness work we do year-round — greener public spaces are also easier to keep clean, and give communities a shared project to take pride in.

Spaces are limited, so if you'd like to join, reach out to us ahead of time and we'll add you to the volunteer list.

Reserve your spot

Let us know you're coming so we can plan supplies and teams.
