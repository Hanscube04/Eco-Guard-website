Who We Are

Built by young people, for the environment

Eco-Guard initiative is a youth-led environmental organization committed to promoting sustainable waste management, empowering young people through green innovation, and transforming public spaces into cleaner, healthier and more sustainable environments.

We work directly with schools, universities, and communities — treating young people not as an audience to educate, but as the workforce that will carry environmental responsibility forward.

A note from our team

Every clean-up, workshop, and Eco-Club we start is designed around one idea: young people don't just need to hear about the environment, they need a real role in protecting it.

— Eco-Guard Initiative Team
