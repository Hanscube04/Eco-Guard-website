Who We Are

An organization built by young people, for the environment.

Eco-Guard initiative is a youth-led environmental organization committed to promoting sustainable waste management, empowering young people through green innovation, and transforming public spaces into cleaner, healthier and more sustainable environments.

More About Us

What drives us

We believe lasting environmental change starts with the people who will inherit it. Every campaign, club, and clean-up we run is designed to hand young people real skills and real responsibility — not just awareness.

— Eco-Guard Initiative
Mission

Why we exist

To promote sustainable environmental practices through waste management, education, and youth empowerment.

Vision

Where we're headed

To build a generation of environmentally responsible individuals committed to creating clean, sustainable, and resilient communities.

Our Work

Three pillars guide everything we do

Sustainable Waste Management

  • Reduce environmental pollution, especially land pollution
  • Promote waste segregation and recycling
  • Convert organic waste into useful products

Youth Empowerment

  • Establish Eco-Clubs in schools and universities
  • Equip youth with practical green skills
  • Create employment opportunities for young people

Environmental Public Awareness

  • Tree planting and community gardening
  • Improving sanitation in target areas
  • Environmental awareness sessions in communities
